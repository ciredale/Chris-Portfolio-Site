VFX

Water, bubbles, and card-physics magic — effects you can feel, tuned to read as gameplay.

FIG. 03.1 — LUCKY DUCKIESWATER · BUBBLES · SPLASH
Water & Bath FX Unreal 5.5 Houdini PC · Mobile

Lucky Duckies

The Signature Feel of Bath-Time Chaos

Water-spray hijinks and over-the-top bath-time chaos give the game its unmistakable look and feel.

  • Spray, bubbles, and splashes built in the Unreal Engine 5.5 pipeline with Houdini.
  • Effects tuned as readable gameplay signals — telegraphing water wheels, launches, and hazards, not just decoration.
  • Authored to hold up across PC and mobile while keeping the bouncy, buoyant tone of the trailer.
FIG. 03.2 — MYTH OF LUMICARD PHYSICS · MAGIC
Card Physics & Magic Unity Physics Dark Fantasy

The Myth
of Lumi

Spectacle You Throw With Your Hands

A 3D physics-based turf war fought by throwing cards — where flight, coverage, and conjured magic are the core spectacle.

  • Card flight and battlefield coverage read as the central visual payoff of every duel.
  • Each summoned Lumi unleashes a distinct magical ability; effects chain together for deck synergy.
  • Staged in the atmospheric dark-fantasy underground city of Prospera — mood carried through effect and light.
